01

The opportunity

Ring 5 is a considered purchase. It costs $399–$499 plus an ongoing membership, it has to fit a finger it has never seen, and its value only becomes clear after weeks of wear. Each of those creates a reason to hesitate. This proposal designs each hesitation out of the page.

Fit

"What if it doesn't fit?"

Risk: buyers guess a size, or leave to find out.

Our answer: sizing is a first-class step, and the kit-first route is the recommended default.

Cost

"What will I actually pay?"

Risk: membership found late feels like a hidden fee, and erodes trust.

Our answer: membership appears in the first screen, the order summary and a cost calculator.

Value

"What will it tell me?"

Risk: sensor specs don't answer "why would I wear this?"

Our answer: lead with the three morning scores and what decisions they help you make.

02

Who we're designing for

Three proto-personas built from the product's positioning. They are assumptions, not research findings, and the first thing we would test in discovery.

Proto-persona A

The sleep-curious professional

Wants
To understand why they wake up tired.
Worries
Paying monthly for data they won't use.
Needs from the page
Plain-language outcomes and the full cost up front.

Proto-persona B

The training-focused athlete

Wants
To know when to push and when to recover.
Worries
Whether Ring 5 is worth it over Ring 4.
Needs from the page
An honest, verified comparison and battery details.

Proto-persona C

The gift buyer

Wants
A thoughtful, premium gift.
Worries
They can't know the recipient's ring size.
Needs from the page
A sizing route that doesn't require knowing the size.

03

Where wearable purchases stall

A heuristic review of common patterns across smart-ring and wearable product pages, mapped to Nielsen's usability heuristics. These are hypotheses to check against Oura's analytics in discovery.

  1. High

    Size is asked after the buyer has committed

    Uncertainty arrives at the worst moment, at checkout, or after delivery as an exchange.

    Error prevention
  2. High

    The subscription appears after the hardware price

    A price that grows late in the journey reads as a hidden cost, even when it's disclosed.

    Visibility of system status
  3. Medium

    Feature-first copy

    Sensor names and metrics don't map to the buyer's question: "what will this change for me?"

    Match with the real world
  4. Medium

    Finish price differences hidden in selectors

    Buyers have to click through options to discover that some finishes cost $100 more.

    Recognition over recall

04

Design principles

  1. Fit before finance. Remove the biggest uncertainty before asking for money.
  2. No surprise costs. Every price, recurring or not, is visible before Add to bag.
  3. Outcomes over sensors. Explain what you'll learn, then how it works.
  4. Calm by default. A wellness product shouldn't feel urgent. No countdowns, no pressure tactics, gentle motion.

06

The key flow: size first

The proposed flow moves the moment of uncertainty to before the ring is made and shipped, and makes it the default path.

Typical wearable flow

  1. Browse
  2. Pick finish
  3. Guess a size
  4. Pay
  5. Ring arrives
  6. Doesn't fit, so exchange

Proposed flow

  1. Browse
  2. Pick finish
  3. "Kit first" (default)
  4. Pay
  5. Try sizes at home
  6. Confirm in app, ring ships

Buyers who already know their Oura size can skip the kit. Choosing "I know my size" reveals a size field, and inline validation stops the order if it's left empty.

07

Key design decisions

  1. Price and membership in the first screen

    The hero states "From $399 · Membership $5.99/month, first month free." Disclosing the full cost early builds trust and removes surprises later.

    See the hero

  2. Finish cards that show the price

    Every finish shows its price on the card, so the $100 premium is visible without clicking. Selection is shown by a filled radio, a heavier border and elevation, never by colour alone. The ring preview updates live.

    Try the finish picker

  3. Sizing kit as the recommended default

    The safest path is preselected and labelled "Recommended", with a one-line reason: fit affects accuracy. That serves the gift buyer and the first-time buyer alike.

    See the size step

  4. "Due today" kept apart from "then"

    The order summary separates the one-time charge from the recurring one, in plain words: "Then $5.99/month for membership, starting after your free month."

    See the summary

  5. An honest ownership calculator

    Buyers can see the 1, 2 or 3-year total. We state that the yearly plan saves only $1.89 a year, rather than implying a bigger discount. Honest numbers make the product easier to trust and to recommend.

    Try the calculator

  6. A comparison with verified rows only

    Where we couldn't confirm a detail, the table says "Check with Oura" rather than guessing. On a health product, a wrong spec costs more than a missing one.

    See the comparison

08

Design system

Type

Newsreader Display · headings, numbers

Inter Interface · body, labels, controls

1.25 modular scale from 13 to 72px. Body text at 17px with 1.6 line-height for comfortable reading.

Colour, with contrast checked

  • Ink16.6:1 on background
  • Ink 27.8:1 · body copy
  • Ink 35.2:1 · captions, control borders
  • Accent7.3:1 · links, white text on it 8.0:1
  • BackgroundWarm ivory, calmer than pure white
  • NightText on it 16.7:1 · for "after dark" stories

Components

  • Radio card
  • Segmented control
  • Order summary
  • Cost calculator
  • Comparison table
  • Accordion
  • Bag drawer
  • Toast
  • Inline error

8-point spacing. 48px minimum height for primary controls. Radii of 10, 16 and 24px.

09

Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA, built in

  • Contrast All text meets 4.5:1 or better; control indicators meet 3:1.
  • Native controls Real radio inputs grouped in fieldsets with legends, so screen readers announce "Finish, Gold, $499, 5 of 6".
  • Visible focus A 3px focus ring on every interactive element, never removed.
  • Target size Interactive targets are at least 44px, beyond the 24px minimum in 2.5.8.
  • Errors Inline, persistent messages linked with aria-describedby, with aria-invalid and focus moved to the field.
  • Live updates Price and summary changes are announced through polite live regions.
  • Dialogs The bag uses a native modal dialog, Esc closes it, and focus returns to the Bag button.
  • Not colour alone Selected states combine shape, weight and elevation.
  • Motion All animation is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Structure A skip link, landmarks, one H1 and a logical heading order.

10

How we'd measure success

We'd set baselines in discovery and agree targets with the team. We won't promise numbers before seeing the data.

Configurator completionVisitors who start "Choose yours" and reach Add to bag
Size-related exchangesShare of orders exchanged for fit, our main "fit before finance" signal
Kit-to-ring timeDays from kit delivery to confirmed size
Membership awarenessA post-purchase survey question: "Did you know about membership before buying?"
Support contactsSizing and billing questions per 1,000 orders

11

Plan & next steps

  1. Week 1Discovery

    Analytics review, stakeholder interviews, 6–8 customer interviews including gift buyers.

  2. Week 2Structure & flows

    Validate personas, finalise IA, map sizing and membership journeys end to end.

  3. Weeks 3–4Design & prototype

    High-fidelity responsive design and a clickable prototype of the purchase flow.

  4. Week 5Usability testing

    5–8 moderated sessions, including screen-reader and keyboard-only participants.

  5. Week 6Handoff

    Design tokens, component specs, annotated accessibility notes and build QA support.

Assumptions to confirm with Oura

  • The Ring 5 size range (the concept uses sizes 4–15, as listed for Ring 4).
  • Sizing kit logistics and timing, and how a gift purchase hands sizing to the recipient.
  • Whether the free first month applies to yearly plans, and regional membership pricing.
  • Which Ring 4 Ceramic accessories, such as the charging case, are included.
