Questions

Before you buy

Do I need a membership?

Yes. Membership unlocks the ring's full features in the Oura app. It's $5.99 a month or $69.99 a year in the US, and your first month is free.

How do I find my size?

Choose "Send me a sizing kit first" when you order. Wear the sample sizes at home, confirm the one that fits in the app, and your ring ships. Oura sizes can differ from jewelry sizes, so the kit is the safest route.

How long does the battery last?

Up to 9 days on a single charge. The travel charging case holds up to a month of charge, so you can leave the cable at home.

Should I get Ring 5 or Ring 4 Ceramic?

Ring 5 is about 40% smaller, with longer battery life and a titanium body, from $399. Ring 4 Ceramic starts at $349 if you prefer a ceramic finish or a lower price.

Is it a medical device?

No. Oura provides wellness insights such as sleep, readiness and blood pressure trends. It doesn't diagnose conditions. Talk to your doctor about any health concerns.
